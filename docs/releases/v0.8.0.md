## Changes in v0.8.0

- **Every device builds its own PDF by default.** The compile mode *This device* is the default, and devices that had the old default (*Automatic*) are moved to it once. Shared builds (*Shared*, *Use peers*) are deprecated: still available in Account > Compiler, but not recommended.
- **Compiler check at every start (desktop).** The app compiles a small document with its compiler. If that fails, it repairs the compiler: it clears the compiler caches, installs the managed compiler again, or sets a broken system TeX aside for the full LaTeX or the quick compiler. Account > Compiler shows the result, with *Check now* and *Use system TeX again*.
- **Long TURN sessions no longer drop after four minutes.** The desktop's TURN client refreshes its channel bindings and permissions the way the relay servers expect.
- **Faster recovery when a connection goes silent on one side.** The other device is woken and accepts the encrypted relay at once, instead of after it notices the loss.
- **Reliable handover to a direct connection.** A new direct link no longer fails with "stale device ticket" when a device refreshed its access during the 8 s check.
- *Test connection* works when both devices had closed their connection to the service: it wakes the co-author first.
- *Compile with my desktop app* in the browser now builds with the desktop app right after pairing.

## Test this release

1. Start the desktop app and open Account > Compiler: the compiler check line should say it passed (or what it repaired). Press *Check now*.
2. Compile a project: the status bar shows *Compile: This device*, and the PDF is built on this computer.
3. With a co-author on another network, keep both apps open for more than five minutes while editing: edits keep arriving within seconds, also on the TURN route.
4. In Sync > Network diagnostics, press *Test connection* while both apps are idle: the co-author answers.
