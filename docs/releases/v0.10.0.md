## Changes in v0.10.0

- **Reliable local LaTeX builds.** The bundled compiler includes a pinned compatible TeX distribution and builds the article and report templates with pdfLaTeX or XeLaTeX, including images, citations and references. Downloaded compiler resources are verified and reusable offline.
- **Choose when to compile.** After changes, Periodic and Manual schedules are saved on each device. Builds share a bounded queue, and a failed input waits for a correction or explicit retry.
- **A steadier editor and PDF preview.** CRLF blank lines retain their line numbers, long PDFs render visible pages first, and older loads cannot replace newer output. SyncTeX follows the accepted source revision and compilation failures preserve the last good PDF.
- **Reachable authoring controls.** Formatting actions move into a keyboard-accessible overflow menu at narrow widths. Save, source sync and local PDF status have separate summaries, with compiler details and recovery actions in Workspace status.
- **Desktop startup and compiler recovery.** Rapid launches and close/reopen cycles retain one owning app, tray and worker. Native package installation shares a deadline with compilation and repairs an interrupted package index on retry.

Required CI now covers focused authoring behavior, real template builds, reviewed visual baselines, long-manuscript stress, native/helper compilation and Windows WebView2 authoring. macOS and Linux WebView acceptance remains outstanding.
